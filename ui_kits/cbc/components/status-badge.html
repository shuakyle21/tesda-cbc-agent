partial is new — the parent CAMS system has no partial concept. It is amber because amber already means “warning tier / moderate urgency”, and “completed with gaps” is exactly that shape. Do not invent a fifth colour for it.

Why retried is amber, not red

A validation retry is the pipeline working — it is the graded conditional branching doing its job. Red would misrepresent correct behaviour as an error. Amber says “watch this”; red says “this is broken.”

Fill alone cannot carry retried vs failed

amber-lt #FCE6CC and red-lt #FCE4E4 differ by a contrast ratio of 1.00. Below: normal, then desaturated. The icon and label must survive the loss of colour — and they do.
